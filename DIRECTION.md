# Design direction — Hurricane Electric

Assigned so that no two of the 25 sites in this campaign converge on the same
look. These are **starting positions with authority**, not idle suggestions: build
from them. You may deviate from any one of them, but the deviation costs you one
line of written justification in `SELECTION-LOG.md` explaining what the assigned
option lost against and why — the same discipline `section-style-repo.md` asks
for everywhere else.

**Primary style family:** dark-moody + minimal-clean
**Page ambition level:** mixed

## The specific risk on this brief

WARNING: the brief fixes Light at #F4F1EA — almost exactly the warm cream front-end-design.md names as an AI-default. It is fixed, so keep it, but do not build the default around it: make the deep navy the dominant field, use the cream as a narrow inset rather than the page background, and keep the amber for marks and rules only. Lexend Deca and Public Sans are both quiet — the type scale has to do the work.

## Carry these through the page

- **Image technique to feature:** Full-Bleed Background Photo with Foreground Card — from `references/image-and-visual-richness.md`. Use it at least twice, deliberately.
- **Motion technique to feature:** Depth Settle on photography, Slow Ambient Drift on the hero scrim — from `references/animation-and-motion-richness.md`.

Both are on top of, not instead of, the per-section choices above. Read the real
entry in the reference file before implementing either — the Avoid-when field
applies to you.
